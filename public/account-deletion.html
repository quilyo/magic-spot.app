Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your MagicSpot Account

You can permanently delete your MagicSpot account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how, what gets removed, and what we briefly retain.

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1.About This Page

This page applies to MagicSpot, a real-time street parking availability app published on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. MagicSpot is operated by the developer behind the magic-spot.com service ("we," "us," "MagicSpot").

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data, contact us at mshelp@magic-spot.com.

2.How to Delete Your Account

You have two ways to request account deletion. Either method permanently removes your account and the personal data described in Section 3.

Option A — Delete in-app (recommended)

  1. Open the MagicSpot app on your Android or iOS device and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu icon in the top-left corner of the map screen.
  3. Select "My Account", then choose "Delete Account".
  4. Type your email address in the confirmation field to verify the request, then tap "Delete".
  5. Your account and the data listed below are removed immediately. You will be signed out and returned to the login screen.

Option B — Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to mshelp@magic-spot.com from the email address registered to your MagicSpot account.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your account email in the body.
  3. We will verify your identity by replying to that email address and complete the deletion within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete.

3.What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove the following from our active systems:

Data TypeStatusWhen
Account email addressDeletedImmediately
Display nameDeletedImmediately
Hashed passwordDeletedImmediately
User profile record (account ID, role, created date)DeletedImmediately
Active session tokens (you are signed out everywhere)DeletedImmediately
Email-confirmation and password-reset records linked to your accountDeletedImmediately

Location data: MagicSpot uses your device's GPS only to display your position on the map while you are using the app. Your GPS coordinates are never stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete on the server side. Any cached map state on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data in your device settings.

4.What Is Briefly Retained, and Why

For security, fraud prevention, and disaster recovery, the following limited information may be retained for a short period after deletion. None of it is used to identify or contact you, and it is permanently purged at the end of the retention window.

Data TypeStatusRetention Period
Encrypted database backups containing your account recordRetainedUp to 30 days, then automatically purged
Authentication and security logs (timestamps, IP addresses of sign-in attempts) — stored by our authentication providerRetainedUp to 90 days, then automatically purged
Email-delivery logs at our email-sending provider (delivery status of past confirmation emails)RetainedUp to 30 days, then automatically purged
Records required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or active fraud investigationRetainedOnly as long as legally required

Backups are encrypted at rest. We do not access them except to recover from a service-wide incident. If a backup is restored after your deletion, your account record is removed again as part of the restoration process.

5.Data MagicSpot Does Not Collect

For clarity, MagicSpot does not collect, store, or share any of the following from end users at any time, so there is nothing to delete in these categories:

  • Phone number, date of birth, postal address, or government identifiers
  • Photos, videos, contacts, microphone or camera input
  • Payment or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers, third-party analytics events, or tracking pixels
  • Stored historical location, parking history, or movement traces

6.Effects of Deletion

  • You will be signed out of MagicSpot on all devices.
  • You will no longer be able to sign in with the deleted email address. To use MagicSpot again, you can create a new account at any time.
  • You will stop receiving any account-related emails from MagicSpot.
  • Deletion cannot be reversed. If you change your mind, you will need to create a new account.

7.Contact

For questions, concerns, or to request a copy of the data associated with your account before deletion, contact us at mshelp@magic-spot.com. We respond within 7 business days.
